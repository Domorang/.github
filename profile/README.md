# Domorang 🏠

**Find, list, and manage rental homes in Nigeria, all in one place.**

Domorang connects renters, agents, and landlords on one platform, so finding a home is simpler and less stressful.

## What we're building
- **For renters:** search listings and find a place without the runaround.
- **For agents:** list properties and reach renters in one place.
- **For landlords:** get your property seen by the right people.

## Where we are
We're an early-stage startup based in Nigeria, currently building our first release for Abuja(we plan to expand with time).
